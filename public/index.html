Frequently Asked Questions
How do I get an invite code?
Invite codes are distributed through the Health1 team, referring surgeons, and our institutional partners. Contact us at robolapcon@health1.in if you don't have one.
Is there a registration fee?
RoboLapCon 2026 is a sponsored, invite-only conference. There is no registration fee for invited delegates.
How are the live surgeries streamed?
Surgeries are performed at Health1 Shilaj Hospital and streamed via HD cameras with professional audio to the conference venue nearby. Faculty commentators provide real-time annotations.
Will I get CME credits?
Yes. We are applying for 6–8 CME credit hours through the Gujarat Medical Council. Certificates will be issued on the same day.
Can I try the robotic console?
Yes. Hands-on dry lab stations will be available during tea breaks. Each delegate gets a 5-minute guided session on the simulator.
What is included for delegates?
Delegate kit, conference badge, breakfast, lunch, high tea, all sessions, dry lab access, CME certificate, and networking opportunities — all included.
Where is the venue?
The conference venue will be a premium banquet hall near Health1 Shilaj Hospital, Ahmedabad. Exact address will be shared with registered delegates via WhatsApp.
