Health1's program is built around that continuity: the same team that manages your pre-transplant workup and surgery also manages your long-term follow-up, working alongside our dialysis partnership (NephroPlus) for patients on the pathway to transplant.

Common Questions

What's the difference between living and deceased donor transplant?

A living donor (often a family member) donates a kidney or part of a liver while alive; a deceased donor transplant relies on organ availability through the national allocation system. Living donor transplants can often be planned and scheduled; deceased donor transplants depend on organ availability.

Is transplant covered under PMJAY?

PM-JAY provides coverage for eligible patients for transplant-related procedures — our team can confirm your specific eligibility and coverage during your evaluation.

What does post-transplant follow-up involve?

Regular monitoring of organ function, immunosuppression medication management, and infection prevention — typically frequent visits in the first months, tapering to periodic check-ups long-term.
